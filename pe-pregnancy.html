Quick reference

Symptoms & signs
  • Dyspnoea · chest pain · haemoptysis
  • Syncope · cough · DVT symptoms
  • HR > 100 · SpO₂ < 90% · RR > 20
  • Low-grade fever · pleural rub
  • Shock · cor pulmonale
Risk factors
  • PMH or FH of VTE
  • Major trauma or surgery ≤ 12 weeks
  • Obesity · immobility ≥ 3 days
  • Increasing age · malignancy
  • Hyperviscosity (myeloma, CML, PV, ET)
  • Thrombophilia
  • Oestrogen therapy (COCP, HRT)
ECG — RH strain
  • Sinus tachycardia
  • S1Q3T3 pattern
  • New RBBB · right axis deviation
  • P pulmonale
LMWH dosing (enoxaparin)
  • Use pre-pregnancy booking weight
  • 1 mg/kg BD (treatment dose)
  • Continue remainder of pregnancy + ≥ 6 weeks postpartum (min total 3 months)
  • Discharge: 4-week course
Alternative diagnoses
  • Pneumonia · pneumothorax
  • ACS · aortic dissection · tamponade
  • Asthma / COPD exacerbation
  • Viral pneumonitis
  • Musculoskeletal chest pain
