Quick reference

Toxic thresholds
  • Acute overdose: ≥ 150 mg/kg risk of toxicity
  • Therapeutic excess: ≥ 75 mg/kg in 24 h
  • High-risk groups: lower thresholds apply
SNAP regimen (overview)
  • Bag 1: 100 mg/kg over 2 hr
  • Bag 2: 200 mg/kg over 10 hr
  • Total course: 12 hr
  • Doses capped at 110 kg
High-risk groups
  • Malnutrition · chronic alcohol excess
  • Eating disorders
  • HIV · cystic fibrosis
  • Enzyme-inducing medications
Bloods bundle
  • Paracetamol concentration (≥ 4 hr post)
  • U+Es, LFTs (especially ALT)
  • INR · VBG
  • PICS/ICE "overdose" bundle
