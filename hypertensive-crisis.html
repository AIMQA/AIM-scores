Quick reference

Definitions
  • Hypertensive emergency: BP ≥ 180/120 with acute EOD
  • Malignant HTN: Grade 3/4 retinopathy ± EOD
  • Acute severe HTN: BP ≥ 180/120, no EOD, normal fundi
Retinopathy grading
  • Grade 1: arteriolar narrowing
  • Grade 2: AV nipping
  • Grade 3: haemorrhages + exudates
  • Grade 4: Grade 3 + papilloedema
Critical drug rules
  • Phaeo: alpha always before beta blockade
  • Aortic dissection: beta-blockade before vasodilator
  • MHT: avoid ACEi/ARB acutely
  • Acute stroke: do not rush BP down
  • SAH: nimodipine for vasospasm prophylaxis
Pregnancy exception

BP ≥ 160/110 in pregnancy may be a medical emergency requiring immediate obstetric assessment, regardless of the standard 180/120 threshold.

Source
