Quick reference

Diagnostic triad
  • D — Glucose ≥ 11 mmol/L (or known diabetes)
  • K — Ketones ≥ 3 mmol/L (or urine ≥ 2+)
  • A — pH < 7.3 or HCO₃ < 18 mmol/L
FRII rates
  • Standard: 0.1 units/kg/hr
  • With 10% glucose (glu ≤ 14): 0.05 units/kg/hr
  • Continue long-acting basal throughout
ITU triggers
  • pH < 7.0 or HCO₃ < 5 mmol/L
  • K⁺ < 3.5 mmol/L on presentation
  • GCS < 12 or AVPU < A
  • SpO₂ < 92% or SBP < 90 mmHg
  • Age < 25 or > 70 years
Resolution criteria
  • Ketones < 0.6 mmol/L
  • pH > 7.3 or venous HCO₃ > 18 mmol/L
  • Patient eating and drinking
